Install catnews

catnews works offline — the pages you visit are cached on your device, and the app shell installs like an app:

Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)

Click the Install app button in the footer — or the ⊕ icon in your browser's address bar.

Android (Chrome)

Open the browser menu (⋮) and tap Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button (⤓) and choose Add to Home Screen.
