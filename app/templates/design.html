Paper & ink

Warm paper canvas, ink-first monochrome, mono-led UI type, serif italics for display. Hairline rules and flat surfaces — after the earendil.com design system. The footer's EAR / KAMI switch flips to the original parchment companion mode.

01 · Manifesto

Design Principles

A small, fixed rule set. Stability comes from repetition, not decoration.

  1. 1

    The canvas is warm paper — never pure white, never cool gray. Light, dark, and pitch themes are first-class tokens, not afterthoughts.

  2. 2

    One accent: ink itself. Links, active states, and emphasis inherit the text color. The source-badge palette is the only chromatic interruption.

  3. 3

    Departure Mono carries function: navigation, labels, badges, chips, code, tabular numbers. Source Serif carries reading: titles, summaries, prose — italic for display moments.

  4. 4

    Separation is a hairline rule at ~18% opacity. No card shadows, no frosted panels, no glow.

  5. 5

    Surfaces are flat and transparent until hover, then a faint wash of ink at 3–5%.

  6. 6

    The hover affordance is a dotted underline. Chrome is fixed to the viewport edges inside one shared frame gutter.

03 · Typography

Type System

Mono carries function, serif carries reading. Two self-hosted families, one scale.

Aa

Mono · Departure Mono

The interface voice: navigation, labels, badges, chips, code, tabular numbers. A pixel font — set at 13–15px for UI text.

Aa

Serif · Source Serif 4

Story titles (weight 600) and reading text. Warm, readable, 300–700 with matching italics.

Aa

Display · Serif Italic

Page titles, section headings, edition subtitles. Weight 400 italic — the earendil-style editorial voice.

07 · Anti-Patterns

What to Avoid

Exceptions are allowed, but the reason should be explicit.

Page canvas

NeverPure white or cool gray as the page background. It makes the warmth of the paper feel brittle.

InsteadAlways warm paper (var(--paper)), with the matching data-theme="dark" or the OLED data-theme="pitch".

Accent

NeverA chromatic accent color competing with the ink. The page is monochrome.

InsteadInk is the accent. Let the source-badge palette carry the only other color, assigned from the fixed 12.

Grays

NeverCool blue-grays (#6b7280, #f3f4f6). They clash with the warm undertone.

InsteadUse the warm alpha ramp of ink: ink → ink-soft → muted → faint.

Shadows & fills

NeverDrop shadows to lift cards, frosted panels, or filled pill badges.

InsteadHairlines and the faint hover wash. Dropdowns may cast one soft shadow — nothing else.
