01 · Manifesto

Design Principles

A small, fixed rule set. Stability comes from repetition, not decoration.

  1. 1

    The canvas is warm parchment, never pure white, never cool gray. Light and dark are first-class tokens, not afterthoughts.

  2. 2

    One ink-blue accent. Source badges are the only chromatic interruption, each drawn from a fixed 12-color palette.

  3. 3

    All grays are warm with a yellow-brown undertone, never cool blue-grays.

  4. 4

    Serif carries hierarchy, mono carries function. Source Serif 4 for headings and body, Fraunces for display, mono for labels, badges, and code.

  5. 5

    Separation is a hairline rule or a whisper shadow, never a hard drop shadow.

  6. 6

    Cards are transparent until hover, then a faint accent wash.

07 · Anti-Patterns

What to Avoid

Exceptions are allowed, but the reason should be explicit.

Page canvas

NeverPure white or cool gray as the page background. It makes the warmth of the parchment feel brittle.

InsteadAlways warm parchment (var(--paper)), with the matching dark theme via data-theme="dark".

Accent

NeverA second chromatic accent color competing with ink blue.

InsteadOne accent; let the source-badge palette carry the only other color, assigned from the fixed 12.

Grays

NeverCool blue-grays (#6b7280, #f3f4f6). They clash with the warm undertone.

InsteadUse the warm ramp: ink → ink-soft → muted → faint, all yellow-brown undertoned.

Shadows

NeverHard drop shadows to lift cards above the page.

InsteadA faint glow, whisper shadow on code, or the accent hover wash. Depth from restraint.
